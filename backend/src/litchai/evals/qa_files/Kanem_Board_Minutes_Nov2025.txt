KANEM LOGISTICS NIGERIA LTD - BOARD MEETING, 4 NOVEMBER 2025

Present: Aisha Kanem (MD), Fatima Kyari (Finance), Ibrahim Sani (Fleet).

1. The board approved the FY2025 accounts timetable: draft accounts to the board by 15 January 2026.
2. The board asked Litch Consulting to confirm if the company still qualifies as a small company for CIT.
3. Fuel costs rose about 18% in Q3. The board wants an analysis per truck before the 2026 budget.
4. A new depot in Potiskum is planned for Q2 2026, cost about NGN 42 million, part funded by a bank loan.
